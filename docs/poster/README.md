## Canonical wording to reuse (video, web summary, report)

- **Question:** Can structured AI instructions produce consistent, checkable market research with
  traceable sources, while leaving judgement to the professional?
- **Corpus:** 324 documents, six markets, 10 May–2 August 2026.
- **Test:** 171 reports graded with a QA rubric. Trade ideas were rebuilt for S&P 500 and FTSE 100
  (57 dates each), using only data available before the trading day.
- **Reports finding:** layout was stronger than calculation and source checks:

  | | Layout | Calculations | Citations |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|
  | S&P 500 | 3.93 | 1.91 | 2.16 |
  | FTSE 100 | 3.68 | 2.11 | 1.44 |
  | Gold | 4.09 | 2.49 | 3.95 |

- **Trade-ideas finding:** strategy results were mixed, and there was no clear improvement at the main
  entry times. Higher QA scores were not associated with stronger trades.
- **Practice:** use with professional review. Synthesis may streamline briefings; savings remain
  unmeasured.
- **CANON:** Constant (locked method) · Architecture (modular) · Normalised (common basis) · Output
  (auditable) · Nomenclature (fixed labels).
