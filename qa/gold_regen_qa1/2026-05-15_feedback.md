   exceeds it by a wide margin.

2. **§11 monthly pivot table is wrong at all 7 levels**, by USD 100–340. Rebuild from the actual April
   2026 H/L/C. Target values (`m_full`): P = 4,673.69 (report 4,648.33), R1 = 4,837.19 (report 4,946.67),

   range width (the report's stated 190-point range width, and its "185-USD band ≈ 3×ATR" claim, both

5. **ATR(14) is never stated as an explicit number anywhere in the report**, in violation of Section 7 row

   claim in the report from the correct value (e.g. Trade 1's "distance 2.23×ATR" is actually 1.37×ATR on

7. **Anchor-override description (§2, §20) misapplies the module's own fixed variable.**

   cards, so this is a *description* defect, not a wrong anchor. Rewrite the §20 log entry to describe
   which time-of-day was used and whether it departs from the previously-populated instance value.

8. **§21b's contango/normalisation claim is unsupported.** §5 states "no basis adjustment applied... gold

   near-zero gap is not evidenced against any of the seven listed sources and should either be dropped or
   supported with the actual settlement-vs-spot delta observed.

## Per-card defects

9. **Card 2026-05-15_Trade_1 — Unit-3 break-even trail is on the wrong side of entry.** Card states
   "Unit 3 SL trails to entry + 0.2R = USD 4,679.60" for a SHORT entered at 4,652 (R = 138). Rule violated:
   card construction rules (reviewer brief §3 / M5): "Unit 3 stop → entry ± 0.2R **in the profitable
   direction** (for a SHORT that is below entry)." A compliant level is entry − 0.2R = 4,652 − 27.60 =
   **USD 4,624.40**, not 4,679.60 (which is above entry and would lock in a loss, not a profit, on a
   SHORT). Not caught by the static linter (CLEAN) because the linter does not check the BE-trail field.

10. **Card 2026-05-15_Trade_2 — same Unit-3 break-even defect.** Card states "entry + 0.2R = USD 4,695.40"
    for a SHORT entered at 4,688 (R = 37). A compliant level is entry − 0.2R = 4,688 − 7.40 = **USD
    4,680.60**.

11. **Card 2026-05-15_Trade_2 — TP3 sits on the wrong side of TP1/TP2** (linter flag `WARN_TP3_ORDER`,
    confirmed by reading the card: TP3 references daily pivot P at 4,665, which for a SHORT from 4,688 is
    numerically *above* TP1 (4,651) and TP2 (4,614) — the runner target would be reached before the earlier
    tranches, not after. Rule violated: TP3 must be beyond TP2 in the trade direction (further from entry
    than TP2, i.e. below 4,614 for this SHORT). Replace the TP3 reference with a level below 4,614, or
    restate it explicitly as a time-stop/alternative rather than a sequential price target.

12. **Card 2026-05-15_Trade_3C — same Unit-3 break-even defect.** Card states "entry + 0.2R = USD 4,585.60"
    for a SHORT entered at 4,570 (R = 78), describing it as locking in "profit at the broken boundary" —
    it does not, since 4,585.60 is above the 4,570 entry. A compliant level is entry − 0.2R = 4,570 − 15.60
    = **USD 4,554.40**.

13. **Card 2026-05-15_Trade_3C — entry, stop and TP1/TP2 are all built on the mis-derived figures in items
    2 and 3 above.** Entry (4,570) = wrong "25-session swing low" (4,585) minus 15; with the corrected
