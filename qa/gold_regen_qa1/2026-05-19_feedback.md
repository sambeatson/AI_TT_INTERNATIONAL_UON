## Per-card defects

9. **Card 2026-05-19_Trade_3A — entry uses the raw 50% Fibonacci retracement (4,617) instead of the
   required midpoint of the 50% and 61.8% retracements.** Rule violated: M5 §5.3a, "Entry | 57.5%
   retracement of the swing (= midpoint of the 50% and 61.8% retracements)." The card's own stated 50%
   (4,617.15) and 61.8% (4,649.40) levels are correct; the entry should be their midpoint. A compliant entry
   is **(4,617.145 + 4,649.325) / 2 = USD 4,633.23**, not 4,617 — off by $16.23.

10. **Card 2026-05-19_Trade_3A — TP1 is set to the swing low itself (4,480.79), not the required 38.2%
    retracement.** Rule violated: M5 §5.3a, "TP1 (Unit 1) | 38.2% retracement — shallow-pullback target."
    The swing low is the far/100% extreme of the swing, not a 38.2% partial retracement. A compliant TP1 is
    **swing_low + 0.382 × (swing_high − swing_low) = 4,480.79 + 0.382 × 272.71 = USD 4,584.97**. What the
    card currently calls TP1 (the full extreme at 4,480.79) is the module's TP2 definition ("0% — full
    retrace, swing origin"), not TP1.

11. **Card 2026-05-19_Trade_3A — stop is set at the 78.6% retracement plus buffer (4,712.75) instead of
    beyond the swing extreme.** Rule violated: M5 §5.3a, "Stop loss | Beyond the 0% anchor (swing high, for
    a short) by 0.25 × ATR14." A compliant stop is **swing_high + 0.25 × ATR14 = 4,753.50 + 0.25 × 104.17 =
    USD 4,779.54** on the `_full` basis (or 4,753.50 + 0.25 × 81.19 = USD 4,773.80 on `_cash`) — both
    materially wider than the 4,712.75 currently used, and both still inside the [ATR_STOP_CAP] 3.5×ATR14
    ceiling from the corrected entry (item 9). The card's current stop is not the module's formula and sits
    closer to price than the rule allows.

12. **Card 2026-05-19_Trade_1 — entry method is a conditional confirmation order, not the fixed "market at
    the daily-open anchor" rule.** The card reads "Sell on first 30-min close back below the 18 May close...
    or market on session open if already below" — a conditional/confirmation entry. Rule violated: reviewer
    brief §3 / M5, "Trade 1: market at the daily-open anchor." State the entry as a market order at the
    00:00 UK (broker 02:00) anchor, or explicitly log the conditional-entry variant as a non-conformance
    against the fixed rule (it is not currently logged as one).
