   **Compliant condition:** the 25 May Open must be within USD 8.88 of 4,543.26 (i.e. in
   [4,534.38, 4,552.14]) to be "consistent," and within USD 19.73 (i.e. in [4,523.53, 4,562.99]) to
   avoid failing outright; the same USD 19.73 band applies around High = 4,579.96 and Low = 4,541.09.
