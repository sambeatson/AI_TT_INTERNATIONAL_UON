   (`data/levels/XAUUSD_by_date/2026-06-04.csv`, `_full` basis — the basis the report itself claims,
   "loco London" / continuous), the true High is 4,496.74, a difference of $44.79 — well beyond the
   Category-3 failure threshold of 0.20×ATR14 (≈ $20.24). A compliant High must sit within
   0.09×ATR14 (≈ $9.11) of 4,496.74, i.e. in [4,487.63, 4,505.85], to be scored "consistent"; up to
   0.20×ATR14 (≈ $20.24), i.e. [4,476.50, 4,516.98], to avoid an outright failure. This single bad
   input is what pushes daily P, R1, R2, R3 and S3 out of tolerance too (see #2) — it is one root
   cause, not five independent errors.
   - The 3-Jun Low (4,439.10 stated vs 4,426.49 true, diff $12.61) and Close (4,440.07 vs 4,434.48
     true, diff $5.59) are both in the recorded-discrepancy band (not failures) — worth tightening but
     not the primary defect.
   - The 3-Jun Open (4,484.86 vs 4,491.84 true, diff $6.98) is within tolerance.

   4,508.03, off by $29.38); R2 = P+(H−L) should be 4,522.82 ± $4.05 (report: 4,576.00, off by
   $53.18); R3 = H+2(P−L) should be 4,548.90 ± $4.05 (report: 4,610.46, off by $61.56); S3 = L−2(H−P)
   should be 4,338.15 ± $4.05 (report: 4,303.17, off by $34.98). S1 (report 4,405.60 vs true 4,408.40,
   diff $2.80) is within tolerance and does not need correction. This is the section to regenerate
   once the H/L/C in #1 is fixed — the P/R/S formulas themselves are applied correctly, only the

3. **§21b Trade 1 — Unit-3 break-even/trail stop is on the adverse side, not the profitable side.**
   The card states "on U2 fill, Unit 3 SL → entry + 0.2R (4,458.4)" for a SHORT with entry 4,440.07
   and R = 92.0. Per the M5 card-construction rule, the Unit-3 stop on fill of Unit 2 must move to
   entry ± 0.2R **in the profitable direction — for a SHORT that is below entry**, i.e.
   entry − 0.2R = 4,440.07 − 18.40 = **4,421.67**, not entry + 0.2R = 4,458.47. As printed, the stop
   sits above entry (still a loss position relative to entry, only smaller than the original 92-point
   risk) rather than locking in a gain. The static linter does not check this arithmetic (it checks
   stop side, TP1 side, TP2 order, zero R — not BE/trail direction), so this card reads CLEAN under
   Card Integrity even though the rule is violated; fix the trail level to 4,421.67.

4. **§21b Trade 3A — the entry and TP1 were computed on the wrong fib anchor, and the fib-anchor low
   is misattributed to the wrong date.** The card fixes its own convention via the TP2 cell, "0% /
   swing low" — i.e. 0% = 4,366.41 (swing low), 100% = 4,595.31 (swing high), width 228.90, with a
   compliant retracement price = low + pct×width. Both the entry and TP1 were instead computed as
   high − pct×width (the mirrored anchor), which silently swaps what each percentage actually means:
   - The entry is labelled "57.5% retracement" at 4,463.69. That price is
     4,595.31 − 0.575×228.90 = 4,463.69 exactly — i.e. it **is** the "57.5% down from the high"
     price, but under the card's own 0%-at-the-low convention that is only
     (4,463.69 − 4,366.41)/228.90 = **42.5%** retraced. A compliant 57.5% entry on the stated
     convention is low + 0.575×228.90 = **4,498.03**.
   - TP1 is labelled "38.2% retrace" at 4,507.87. That price is 4,595.31 − 0.382×228.90 = 4,507.87
     exactly — the same mirrored-anchor error — which under the card's own convention is
     (4,507.87 − 4,366.41)/228.90 = **61.8%** retraced, not 38.2%. This is also the direct cause of
     the linter's DUD_TP1_SIDE flag: at 4,507.87 the "first target" sits **above** the 4,463.69 short
     entry, i.e. on the adverse side. A compliant TP1 on the stated convention is
     low + 0.382×228.90 = **4,453.85**, which is correctly below entry either way (whether entry is
     left at 4,463.69 or corrected to 4,498.03).
   - The fib-anchor cell names the low "28 May/2-3 Jun low 4,366.41," but the report's own §6 table
     shows the 2–3 Jun low as **4,439.10**, and 4,366.41 as the **28 May** low only. Drop the "2-3 Jun"
     attribution from this cell — it contradicts the report's own §6 data.
