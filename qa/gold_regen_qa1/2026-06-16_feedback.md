## Report-level defects

   "Daily (prior session 12 Jun)," and its P (4,211.96) reproduces exactly from the 12 Jun H/L/C
   already shown in the report's own §6. D-1 for session 16 Jun is **15 Jun (Monday)**, whose OHLC the
   report's own §6 has already validated (High 4,345.40 / Low 4,211.80 / Close 4,314.33) — 12 Jun is
   three sessions stale. Checked against the file's correctly-dated `d_full_*` pivots (built from
   15 Jun), all seven daily levels fail by USD 55–137, vastly exceeding the USD 14.12
   (0.115×ATR14) failure threshold: P 4,211.96 vs 4,314.77 (diff 102.81), R1 4,253.89 vs 4,363.92
   (diff 110.03), S1 4,177.38 vs 4,260.03 (diff 82.65), R2 4,288.47 vs 4,418.66 (diff 130.19),
   S2 4,135.45 vs 4,210.88 (diff 75.43), R3 4,330.40 vs 4,467.81 (diff 137.41), S3 4,100.87 vs
   4,156.14 (diff 55.27). By contrast the weekly table ("W/E 12 Jun," correctly the last complete
   week before D) matches the file's `w_full_*` almost exactly (largest diff USD 1.97, all seven
   consistent), and the monthly table (May) matches `m_full_*` almost exactly (largest diff USD
   0.56) — so this is a sourcing error isolated specifically to the daily pivot's input session, not
   a systemic sourcing failure. **Rule violated:** brief §4 pivot tolerance table; M2 Research
   Standard evidence grounding; brief §2 row 3.4 ("§11's daily/weekly/monthly pivots... against the
   level file"). **Compliant condition:** rebuild the daily table from the file's `d_full_P/R1/S1/R2/S2/R3/S3`
   = 4,314.77 / 4,363.92 / 4,260.03 / 4,418.66 / 4,210.88 / 4,467.81 / 4,156.14 (each within USD 3.95
   of the report's own 15 Jun H/L/C-based recomputation), and re-source it explicitly from D-1 (15 Jun),
   not a stale 12 Jun snapshot.
2. **15 June Open and Low are Category-3 failures against the level file.** §6 states Open 4,219.32
   (equal to 12 Jun's close — itself evidence the pivot-input confusion in defect 1 runs through the
   whole date block) and Low 4,211.80. The file gives `prev_open_full` = 4,268.73 (diff USD 49.41,
   exceeds the USD 24.55 failure threshold) and `prev_low_full` = 4,265.62 (diff USD 53.82, exceeds

   within USD 11.05 of 4,268.73 (consistent) or at least within USD 24.55 (to avoid failing); the
   same band applies around Low = 4,265.62.
