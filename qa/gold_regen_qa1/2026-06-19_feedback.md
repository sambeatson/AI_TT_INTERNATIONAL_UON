# Feedback — Gold_Report_19-Jun-2026.md (XAUUSD, D = 2026-06-19)

Numbered, specific, actionable. Numbers are drawn from `data/levels/XAUUSD_by_date/2026-06-19.csv`
(leak-free, `last_bar_date` 2026-06-18 < D). No statement below concerns price on or after D.

## Report-level defects

1. **AS_OF_DATE is set to D instead of D−1.** §2 states "As-of date... 19 June 2026" but
   `M1_Variables_v2_1.md` fixes AS_OF_DATE to "the last completed regular session... that closed
   strictly before the report date." For D = 2026-06-19, AS_OF_DATE must be 2026-06-18. Fix: set
   §2's as-of date to 18 June 2026 and treat 19 June as the trading/session date only, not as a
   completed, sourceable session.

2. **§6's 19-Jun (D) row is presented as settled/"CORROBORATED" data, which is inconsistent with
   Trade 1 entering on 19 June.** Because D is mis-treated as already-completed (defect 1), the
   Exec Snapshot, Consensus Price Call and §6 all quote a full 19-Jun close ($4,137.80) as fact,
   while §21b's Trade 1 card enters "Market at 07:00 UK... Reference 4,188.30 (19 Jun open)" — an
   entry that, on 19 June itself, cannot yet know that day's close. Compounding this, §21c's
   "5-Session **No-Leakage** Backtest" lists Fri 19 Jun as a resolved t−1 trade (entry 4188.3 / exit
   4137.8, "+1.0 TP1", "Days to res.: 1") — the identical entry that the live Trade 1 card is
   proposing for that same day. Fix: the backtest window must run through 18 June only (t−1 = 18
   Jun), and D's own session must never appear as realised OHLC or a resolved trade outcome.

3. **Consensus price build uses 2 sources, not the required ≥6.** §5 states the weighted-median
   consensus of $4,139/oz is "anchored on two independent spot sources" (Investing.com, Trading
   Economics); the other four listed sources in §4 are explicitly downweighted to
   "directional... not blended into the spot number." `M1_Variables_v2_1.md` §C fixes
   MINIMUM_SOURCE_COUNT = 6 "for consensus price build." Fix: either include enough additional
   independent spot (not futures/dealer-commentary) sources in the blended consensus, or state
   explicitly why the fixed minimum does not apply to this run.

4. **§9 "Medium-Term Regime & VOLator" has no content.** The heading exists but is followed
   immediately by §10 — no regime call, no persistence/overlap discussion, no VOLator readout, no
   KER derivation — despite §1 asserting "medium-term regime is Transitional — Bearish" and
   "Kaufman efficiency classifies the move as Trending Down — Strong (smoothed KER ≈ −0.35)." Fix:
   populate §9 with the regime classification, its persistence/overlap evidence and the VOLator
   comparative-volatility readout that the module requires, and reconcile it explicitly against
   §8's "Trending" short-term label (the two are not the same regime and the report never explains
   the difference).

5. **§7 "Charts" has no per-chart evidence.** All five chart toggles
   (CHART_CANDLESTICK/OPEN_ZERO/5WEEK/VOLATOR/PIVOT) default YES per M1, but §7 contains only one
   generic note about volatility scaling — no caption or placeholder for any individual chart. A
   caption or placeholder per chart is acceptable evidence per the review protocol; an empty section
   is not. Fix: add one caption/placeholder line per toggled chart.

6. **§20 "Agent Log" is empty.** `M1_Variables_v2_1.md`'s DAILY_OPEN_ANCHOR rule requires that any
   departure from the instance's populated anchor value be "recorded as such... in the handoff
   record" — the Agent Log is that record. Trade 1's entry field does flag "(anchor override)," but
   nothing is logged in §20 to confirm what the populated anchor was, why it was overridden, or that
   this is a non-conformance rather than the instance's anchor value. Fix: add an §20 entry stating
   the populated anchor, the override applied, and the reason.

7. **Daily pivot table (§11) shows 5 levels per side (R5→S5); weekly and monthly tables show only
   R3..S1/S2 (missing S3 on the downside).** The protocol's Section 7 checklist (2.2) specifies
   "pivot tables ordered R3→P→S3 (three levels each side)" for daily, weekly, and monthly alike.
   Fix: standardise all three pivot tables to R3→P→S3.

   losing direction for a short. A compliant TP1 for this SHORT must be **below** entry

   4,121.86→4,382.85 swing), the correct 38.2% level is 4,121.86 + 0.382×(4,382.85−4,121.86) =
   **4,221.51**, not 4,283.16 (which is instead the ~61.8% level of the swing, i.e. mislabelled).
   Card Integrity for this card is floored to 60 (100 − 40×1 DUD) purely on this defect. Fix:
   replace TP1 with the correctly-signed 38.2% retracement value (≈4,221.51), below entry and above
   TP2 (4,121.86), preserving TP1<entry<... ordering for a SHORT.
