# Feedback — Gold_Report_24Jun2026.md (24 June 2026)
Numbered, specific, actionable. Numbers below come from `data/levels/XAUUSD_by_date/2026-06-24.csv`
(basis `_full`, since the report claims spot/loco-London/continuous). No statement below concerns price
behaviour on or after 24 June.

## Report-level defects

1. **ATR(14) is stated as ≈$52.7/oz (§9, §20) but the leak-free level file gives 122.3607 (full) /
   102.8557 (cash) — off by more than 55% either way.** Rule: brief §4 tolerance table, ATR14 row
   ("consistent ≤10% relative … failure >25% relative"). A compliant report must state and show the
   ATR(14) derivation from the validated 5-day close series and land within 10% of 122.3607 (i.e.
   110.1–134.6) on a full-day basis, or of 102.8557 (i.e. 92.6–113.1) if it is explicitly computed on
   the cash-session series and labelled as such. This error propagates directly into the Trade 1 stop
   buffer and the "3×ATR" runner cap in §21b.

2. **The 23-Jun Close is stated as 4,129.07 (§4, §6) against a level-file close of 4,110.32 — a gap of
   18.75, which exceeds the close tolerance of 0.115×ATR14 = 14.07 (brief §4).** This is a Category 3
   failure, not a basis difference (no futures/contango label is attached to this figure, so the
   contango exception does not apply). A compliant report must reconcile its consensus 23-Jun close to
   within 14.07 of 4,110.32 (full) or, if the report is deliberately built on the cash session and says
   so, within 11.83 of 4,111.57 (cash).

3. **Daily pivots S1/S2/S3 (§11: 4,093.65 / 4,058.22 / 4,007.05) all fail the level-file check.**
   Level-file `_full` values: S1 4,067.9967 (diff 25.65), S2 4,025.6733 (diff 32.55), S3 3,960.4967
   (diff 46.55) — all exceed the 14.07 failure threshold. Daily P and R1 are within tolerance (diffs
   11.65 and 4.75); R2 is a recorded discrepancy (diff 9.25, inside the 4.89–14.07 band) but not a
   failure. The S-side errors trace to the wrong 23-Jun Low/Close feeding the P=(H+L+C)/3 formula
   (defect 2 above): a compliant daily pivot table recomputed from the level file's own H=4,198.35,
   L=4,090.85, C=4,110.32 gives P=4,133.1733, S1=4,067.9967, S2=4,025.6733, S3=3,960.4967,
   R1=4,175.4967, R2=4,240.6733 — use these as the target values once the underlying OHLC is fixed.

4. **The weekly pivot table (§11: P 4,221.33, WR1 4,321.78, WR2 4,483.29, WS1 4,059.82, WS2 3,959.37)
   is accurate** — every level is within tolerance of the level file's `w_full_*` values (largest gap
   3.75 on WR1, all ≤ 0.04×ATR = 4.89). No action needed here; flag this as evidence the weekly
   construction is sound even though the daily construction (defect 3) is not.

5. **§11 pivot tables are structurally incomplete against the required "R3→P→S3, three levels each
   side, daily/weekly/monthly" format (brief §2, row 2.2).** The daily table omits R3 (only R1, R2 on
   the resistance side); the weekly table omits both WR3 and WS3; **the monthly pivot table is missing
   entirely.** A compliant §11 needs three tables (daily, weekly, monthly), each with all six outer
   levels plus P. Monthly full-basis values are available for reconstruction: P=4,559.9033,
   R1=4,753.4567, R2=4,966.9233, R3=5,160.4767, S1=4,346.4367, S2=4,152.8833, S3=3,939.4167.

6. **§13d (Divergence) is printed before §13c (News calendar); the required order is
   13a→13b→13c→13d** (brief §2, row 2.1). Move the Divergence paragraph after the news-calendar table.

7. **§20 contains the bracketed module-variable name "[daily-open anchor]" in the report body**, which
   is a direct breach of the restriction "no bracketed variable names, no module codes (M1..M5), no
   framework name in the report body" (brief §2, row 5.4 / framework §6 restriction-breach override).
   §19's phrase "the framework's no-synthesis discipline" is a softer, generic version of the same
   issue. Rewrite both without bracket syntax or references to the underlying template/framework.

8. **§20's "override applied" note for the daily-open anchor discloses the wrong thing.** It states that
   00:00 UK is being used as the anchor (i.e. no time-of-day departure) but never discloses that the
   Trade 1 MARKET entry price (4,198.66) is not the price at that anchor — see defect 9. A compliant
   non-conformance note must name the specific value that departed (the entry price used vs. the true
   anchor price) and the size of the departure, not restate the anchor time as unchanged.

9. **Only 5 distinct sources (Investing.com, TradingEconomics, Goldpedia, LiteFinance, TradingView)
   feed the §4 price-consensus table**, against the ≥6-independent-sources requirement (brief §2, row
   1.1). CoinCodex is used only for sentiment (§13a), not price. Add a sixth independent price source
   to §4, or reclassify one of the currently sentiment-only sources into the price-consensus build with
   a quoted OHLC figure.

## Card-level defects

10. **Trade 1 — MARKET entry (4,198.66) does not equal the D−1 close.** Rule (brief §3, static
    integrity): "a MARKET entry equals the D−1 close." The report's own 23-Jun close is 4,129.07; the
    level file's is 4,110.32 (full) / 4,111.57 (cash). 4,198.66 is actually the in-progress 24-Jun
    print (labelled "live, partial" in §4/§6), not the D−1 close. A compliant Trade 1 entry must be set
    to 4,110.32 (full) or 4,111.57 (cash) — whichever basis the report commits to — not to an
    intra-session read of the day already in progress.

11. **Trade 1 — stop-loss anchor (5-day swing high 4,330.39) is understated against the level file's
    `swing_high_5d_full` of 4,382.22 (diff 51.83).** Rule: brief §3, "Stop = tighter of (5-day swing
    extreme, nearest S/R) + 0.25×ATR buffer." A compliant stop must be built from 4,382.22, not
    4,330.39 — recompute once the ATR(14) figure (defect 1) is also corrected, since the buffer term
    (0.25×ATR) is currently using the wrong ATR as well.

12. **Trade 2 does not follow the M5 construction rule for its stated regime.** §9 states the
    medium-term regime as TREND_DOWN. Rule (brief §3): "TREND → entry at P + 0.10×(R1−P) (long) [mirror
    for short: P − 0.10×(P−S1)], stop P − 0.8×(P−S1) [mirror: P + 0.8×(R1−P)], TPs R1/R1.5/R2 [mirror:
    S1/S1.5/S2]." Using the report's own weekly P=4,221.33, R1=4,321.78, S1=4,059.82, a compliant
    TREND-regime short entry is 4,221.33 − 0.10×(4,221.33−4,059.82) = 4,205.18, with stop
    4,221.33 + 0.8×(4,321.78−4,221.33) = 4,301.69, and TPs tiered at S1/S1.5/S2 (4,059.82 /
    ~3,989.82-region / 3,959.37) — not the pivot-fade construction currently used (entry at P itself,
    stop 4,265.00 with no stated derivation, TPs mixing daily P and daily S1 from a different horizon
    than the entry basis).

13. **Trade 2 is missing required fields**: no R stated (card JSON `card_R_points: null`), no TP3, no
    runner rule, no tranche/management text. Rule (brief §3): "Three equal units on every card… TP1 =
    entry ± 1R, TP2 = entry ± 2R for … RANGE Trade 2." Whichever regime formula applies (defect 12),
    the card must state R explicitly and derive TP1/TP2 from it (RANGE case) or state the tiering
    explicitly (TREND case), plus a stated runner/management clause.

14. **Trade 3A is built as a breakout-stop at the prior low, not as the required 57.5%-retracement
    construction.** Rule (brief §3): "Trade 3A (trend): entry 57.5% retrace of a qualifying swing
    (magnitude ≥2×ATR, 4–10 session lookback, endpoints logged), stop beyond the 0% anchor by 0.25×ATR,
    TP1 38.2%, TP2 0%, TP3 100%+ extension." The card as written (entry 4,109.40 "on confirmed break of
    23-Jun low", stop 4,200.50, TP1 4,060.00 labelled "38.2%/structural", TP2 4,007.05 labelled "daily
    S3 / runner") never logs the qualifying swing's two endpoints, never shows the 57.5% retracement
    entry calculation, and has no TP3 at a 100%+ extension. A compliant Trade 3A must state the swing
    high/low used (with dates), confirm |high−low| ≥ 2×ATR14, and derive entry/stop/TP1/TP2/TP3 from
    the stated retracement percentages against those two endpoints.

15. **Trade 1 TP2 arithmetic is off by $0.01** (card states 3,908.85; entry 4,198.66 − 2×144.90 =
    3,908.86). Immaterial in isolation but should be corrected once the entry itself (defect 10) is
    fixed, since TP2 will move regardless.
