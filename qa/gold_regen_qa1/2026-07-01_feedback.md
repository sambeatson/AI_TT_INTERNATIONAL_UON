   construction rule.** The M5 rule for a TREND regime is: entry at P ± 0.10×(P−S1 or R1−P depending on

   at a flat limit on R1 ($4,077.64) with a structural (5-day-swing) stop and fixed ±1R/±2R targets
   ($4,040.02 / $4,002.40) — closer to the RANGE-short pattern (limit at a resistance pivot), but even then
   without the RANGE rule's tiered stop (3.5×ATR or structural+0.25×ATR) or tiered pivot targets. Using the
   file's own daily pivots and the TREND-short mirror of the stated rule: entry = P − 0.10×(P−S1) =
   $4,004.70 − 0.10×$58.74 = **$3,998.83**; stop = P + 0.8×(R1−P) = $4,004.70 + 0.8×$61.62 = **$4,054.00**;
   TP1 = S1 = **$3,945.97**, TP1.5 = mid(S1,S2) = **$3,915.16**, TP2 = S2 = **$3,884.34**. This is the
   construction a TREND-regime Trade 2 must follow; the report's current card follows neither this rule nor
