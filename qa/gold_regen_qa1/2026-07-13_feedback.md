   from its stated pivots, is H≈4,244.9 / L≈4,057.9 — a range that does not match either the level
   file's week or the report's own §6 5-day OHLC, whose Mon–Fri high/low span is only 4,058–4,172). A

   negative score, which would still fall below the 0.25 |threshold| and still suppress Trade 1, but for

## Card-level defects

6. **Trade 2 (Pivot, LONG) — stop lacks the required ATR buffer.** Card: entry 4,085.0, stop 4,059.0
   (linter: `WARN_R_TINY(0.26xATR)`, R = 26.0 USD/oz = 0.255×ATR14_full). The card's own stated anchor
   is "below 8 Jul swing low 4,058," i.e. only 1.0 USD/oz beyond the structural anchor. The rule for a
   RANGE-regime Trade 2 stop is structural anchor **+ 0.25×ATR14** (≈ 25.47 USD/oz at ATR14_full =
   101.8964), not structural anchor + a nominal 1 USD/oz. A compliant stop must sit at or below
   4,058 − 25.47 ≈ 4,032.5 (for a long), which would also clear the 0.3×ATR14 ≈ 30.6 USD/oz minimum-R
   floor the linter is warning against.
7. **Trade 2 — TP1 mislabelled as +1.0R.** Card states TP1 = 4,110.0 "+1.0R (250 ticks)" while the
   card's own stated R = 26.0 USD/oz (260 ticks) and 4,110.0 − 4,085.0 = 25.0 USD/oz (250 ticks) =
   0.96R, not 1.0R. Either restate TP1's R-multiple as 0.96R, or move TP1 to entry + 1.0×R = 4,085.0 +
   26.0 = 4,111.0 to match the stated multiple.
8. **Trade 3B (Mean-Reversion, SHORT) — stop lacks the required ATR buffer.** Card: entry 4,146.0, stop
   4,174.0 (linter: `WARN_R_TINY(0.27xATR)`, R = 28.0 USD/oz = 0.275×ATR14_full). Anchor is "above 6–7
   Jul highs 4,170–4,172" — only ≈2.0–4.0 USD/oz beyond the structural anchor, again short of the
   required 0.25×ATR14 ≈ 25.47 USD/oz buffer. A compliant stop must sit at or above 4,172 + 25.47 ≈
   4,197.5 (for a short), clearing the same 0.3×ATR14 ≈ 30.6 USD/oz minimum-R floor.
9. **Trade 3B — TP1 sign notation inconsistent with TP2.** Card states TP1 = 4,118.0 "(−1.0R, 280
   ticks)" for a SHORT position where TP1 is in-profit, while TP2 is labelled "+2.0R" using the opposite
   sign convention for the same profitable direction. Standardise the sign convention so both TPs use
   the same polarity for "in the direction of profit" (e.g. both positive, or both signed by raw price
   change direction).
