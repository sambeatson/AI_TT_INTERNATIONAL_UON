# Feedback — Gold_Report_15Jul2026.md (15 July 2026)
Numbered, specific, actionable. Numbers below come from `data/levels/XAUUSD_by_date/2026-07-15.csv`
(basis `_full`, since the report claims spot/loco-London/continuous). No statement below concerns price
behaviour on or after 15 July.

## Report-level defects

1. **§11's Daily pivot table is computed from the wrong prior session.** It is explicitly labelled
   "Daily (prior session 13 Jul)", but D−1 for a 15-Jul report is 14 Jul — which §6's own OHLC table
   correctly uses as the most recent completed session. Recomputing P=(H+L+C)/3 from the level file's
   14-Jul H=4,103.17/L=3,983.65/C=4,052.87 gives **P=4,046.5633, R1=4,109.4767, S1=3,989.9567,
   R2=4,166.0833, S2=3,927.0433, R3=4,228.9967, S3=3,870.4367** — use these as the target values. The
   report's printed table (P=4,033.6, R1=4,080.6, S1=3,954.2, R2=4,160.0, S2=3,907.2, R3=4,207.0,
   S3=3,827.8) fails tolerance (brief §4, pivot row: failure > 0.115×ATR14 = 11.62) on 6 of 7 levels;
   only R2 (diff 6.08) sits inside the discrepancy band. Rule: brief §2 row 3.4, "external: §11's
   daily/weekly/monthly pivots... against the level file."

2. **The 14-Jul Close is stated as 4,074.6 (§4, §6) against a level-file close of 4,052.87 — a gap of
   21.73, exceeding the close-tolerance failure threshold of 0.115×ATR14 = 11.62** (brief §4). No
   futures/contango label is attached to this figure, so the contango exception does not apply. A
   compliant report must reconcile its consensus 14-Jul close to within 11.62 of 4,052.87 (full), or
   within 8.86 (0.115×75.7821) of 4,055.78 if deliberately built on the cash session and labelled so.

3. **RSI2 for 14 Jul is stated as 55.6 against a level-file `rsi2_full` of 30.5007 — a gap of 25.1,
   exceeding the failure threshold of 15 points** (brief §4, RSI2 row). Rule: a compliant RSI2 for
   14 Jul must land within 15 of 30.5007 (full) or 33.5517 (cash), i.e. roughly 15.5–45.5 (full) or
   18.6–48.6 (cash), and the report should show its RS/RSI2 derivation (brief §2 row 3.3) rather than
   present the figure unworked.

4. **§11's Monthly pivot table is wrong by USD 52–220 on every level it shows, and two levels are
   missing outright.** Report values (P=4,113.6, R1=4,277.7, R2=4,547.8, S1=3,843.5, S2=3,679.4) vs
   level file (`m_full_P`=4,165.5133, `m_full_R1`=4,387.9467, `m_full_R2`=4,768.3033,
   `m_full_S1`=3,785.1567, `m_full_S2`=3,562.7233) — diffs of −51.91 / −110.25 / −220.50 / +58.34 /
   +116.68, all far beyond the 11.62 failure threshold. `m_full_R3`=4,990.7367 and `m_full_S3`=3,182.3667
   are the missing outer levels (see defect 5). Every monthly figure needs rebuilding from these values.

5. **§11's Monthly and Daily pivot tables are structurally incomplete against the required "R3→P→S3,
   three levels each side" format (brief §2, row 2.2).** The Daily and Weekly tables are correctly
   complete (7 rows each); the Monthly table gives only R2/R1/P/S1/S2 — R3 and S3 are absent. A
   compliant §11 needs all three tables with all six outer levels plus P; use
   R3=4,990.7367 / S3=3,182.3667 (full basis) to complete the monthly table once defect 4 is fixed.

6. **§6, §7, §8, §9, §10, §11, §12, §14, and the master §20/§21 headings are entirely missing from the
   document.** Only §1–§5 and §15–§19 carry the required "# N · Title" heading; the remaining sections'
   content exists only as unheaded tables/bullet lists (or, for §13/§21, only as sub-labelled
   13a–13d/21a–21d text with no parent "§13"/"§21" heading). Both `Gold_Report_13Jul2026.md` and
   `Gold_Report_14Jul2026.md` carry all 21 numbered headings, so this is a report-specific regression,
   not a template characteristic. Rule: brief §2 row 2.1, "all 21 top-level sections present and
   ordered." Add the missing headings in the correct numeric positions; do not renumber or reorder the
   existing content, which is otherwise in the right place.

7. **ATR(14) is never stated as an explicit figure anywhere in the report.** It is only inferable
   indirectly — e.g. Trade 2's stop buffer (4,033.6−4,008.5=25.1, implying ATR≈100.4) and Trade 3C's
   "≥25 USD (0.25×ATR)" (implying ATR≈100) — both of which happen to sit within 10% of the level file's
   `atr14_full`=101.0786. Rule: brief §2 row 3.3, "ATR(14) stated." State the ATR14 figure explicitly
   (target ≈101.08, or ≈75.78 if the cash-session basis is used and labelled) and derive the buffer
   terms from it directly rather than leaving the value implicit.

   GC=F "trades a few dollars above loco-London spot on carry" and the footnote calls it "a small basis
   adjustment" — no USD figure is given. Rule: brief §2 row 5.2, "the futures-to-spot normalisation
   stated with its size." State the specific USD adjustment applied to the 14-Jul CME GC=F quote
   (4,071.3 → normalized ~4,071) so the adjustment is reproducible.

9. **§20 (unheaded — see defect 6) contains the bracketed module-variable name "[DAILY_OPEN_ANCHOR]"
   verbatim** ("Overrides this run: [DAILY_OPEN_ANCHOR] set to 07:00 UK (baseline 00:00 UK)..."), and
   §5's footnote references a module code directly ("documented as an assumption (M2 data-quality
   rule)"). §21b's "Actionable now" paragraph also references "the framework's automated cards" (a
   softer, generic version of the same issue). Rule: brief §2 row 5.4, "no bracketed variable names, no
   module codes (M1..M5), no framework name in the report body" — this is the restriction-breach

   underlying template/module architecture — e.g. "Daily-open anchor overridden to 07:00 UK this run
   (baseline 00:00 UK), logged as a non-conformance" and "documented as an assumption (data-quality
   rule)".

## Card-level defects

10. **Trade 2 — TP3 (4,294.9) sits below TP2 (4,343.9), a non-monotonic ladder** (linter
    WARN_TP3_ORDER, `qa/gold_regen_qa1/lint_static/2026-07-15.csv`, confirmed verbatim). Rule: brief §3
    static integrity, "TP3 beyond TP2 or null." Either state TP3 as null (the runner simply trails
    without a fixed target) or set it beyond TP2 (4,343.9) in the trade's direction — Weekly R2
    (4,295.5233 from the level file, closely matching the report's own quoted 4,294.9) is not a valid
    TP3 anchor here since it sits inside the TP1–TP2 band, not beyond it.

11. **Trade 2's entry (4,120.3) and stop (4,008.5) are both derived from the mis-dated Daily pivot table
    (defect 1).** Entry = Daily R1.5 = (Daily R1 + Daily R2)/2 = (4,080.6+4,160.0)/2 = 4,120.3 using the
    report's own (wrong) daily table; stop = Daily P (4,033.6) − 0.25×ATR. Once the Daily pivot table is
    corrected to the 14-Jul basis (P=4,046.5633, R1=4,109.4767, R2=4,166.0833), a compliant entry is
    R1.5 = (4,109.4767+4,166.0833)/2 = 4,137.78, and a compliant stop is P − 0.25×ATR14 =
    4,046.5633 − 0.25×101.0786 = 4,021.29 (full basis) — both entry and stop, and therefore R, TP1
    and TP2, need recomputation from the corrected pivot table.

12. **Trade 2's tick count does not match its own stated R.** The card gives "Risk R = 111.8 USD
    (11,177 ticks at 0.01)"; 111.8 / 0.01 = 11,180, not 11,177. Rule: brief §3, "stops and targets in
    both price and native units" — the native-unit conversion must be arithmetically correct. Use
    11,180 ticks (or recompute once defect 11's corrected R is in place).

13. **Trade 2's stop and targets are given only in price, not also in native (tick) units** — only the
    aggregate R (111.8 USD) is tick-converted, not the individual stop/TP1/TP2/TP3 levels. Rule: brief
    §3, "stops and targets in both price and native units." Add the tick-equivalent of each level
    (entry, stop, TP1, TP2) alongside its price.

14. **Trade 3C's contingent-long stop (4,123.1, "range-low +0.40×width") is anchored to the wrong
    boundary.** The contingent long arms on a confirmed close *above* the range high (4,383.6 + 0.25×ATR
    ≈ 4,408.7); its stop should reference that same broken boundary (the range high, now acting as
    support), not the opposite range-low boundary (3,949.4). As printed, the stop sits ~285 USD below
    the 4,408.7 entry — near the 3.0×ATR14 static-integrity cap (303.24) and structurally inconsistent
    with a 0.40×width buffer measured from the correct side. Rule: brief §3, "3C (transition)... stop at
    the boundary + 0.40×width" — "the boundary" is the one the close broke through. Since this trade is
    not yet armed, this only needs correcting before the card could ever be triggered, not urgently.

15. **The §11 "confluence zone... where Weekly P and Monthly P nearly coincide" (§11 narrative) is an
    artefact of the Monthly-pivot error (defect 4).** The report's own Weekly P (4,114.7, which
    reconciles cleanly to the level file's `w_full_P`=4,114.5633) and its stated Monthly P (4,113.6) do
    sit close together, but the level file's correct `m_full_P` is 4,165.5133 — roughly 51 away from
    Weekly P, not a near-coincidence. Once the Monthly pivot table is corrected (defect 4), this
    "confluence" claim in the §11 position narrative should be removed or re-derived, and Trade 2's
    entry rationale (defect 11), which partly cites this same confluence, should be revisited alongside
    it.
