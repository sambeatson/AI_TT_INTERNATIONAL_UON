# Feedback — Gold_Report_16Jul2026.md (16 July 2026)
Numbered, specific, actionable. Numbers below come from `data/levels/XAUUSD_by_date/2026-07-16.csv`
(basis `_full`, since the report's own 15-Jul OHLC matches `_full` far better than `_cash` — e.g. stated
Low 4,017.50 vs `prev_low_full` 4,017.44). No statement below concerns price behaviour on or after 16 July.

## Report-level defects

1. **Bracketed module-variable names and M1–M5 module codes appear throughout the report body — at
   least 25 instances — including the title line and the footer**, e.g. "Modular Prompt Architecture
   v2.1 · M1 Gold instance · M5 Strategies ENABLED" (line 5), "[PRIMARY_ASSET]", "[AS_OF_DATE]",
   "[DAILY_OPEN_ANCHOR]", "[CONSENSUS_METHOD]", "[MINIMUM_SOURCE_COUNT]", "[FIB_RANGE_DAYS]",
   "[CONVICTION_THRESHOLD]", "[W_SHORT_TECH]", "[W_MEDIUM_REGIME]", "[MAX_SIMULTANEOUS_LONG_SHORT]",
   "[BACKTEST_LOOKBACK_DAYS]", and "M2 §A.2", "M3 §2a", "M4 §12", "M5 §5.1", "M5 §4a/4b/4d" scattered
   through §3, §5, §8, §10, §12, §13, §20 (five separate log entries) and §21a/§21b. Rule: brief §2 row
   5.4, "no bracketed variable names, no module codes (M1..M5), no framework name in the report body."
   A compliant report states these as plain prose ("the asset", "the as-of date", "the daily-open
   anchor", "the consensus method", "the minimum source count", "the Fibonacci lookback window", "the
   conviction threshold", "per Strategy Rule 5.1") with no bracket syntax, no M-numbering, and no
   reference to "Modular Prompt Architecture" anywhere in the body — the title/footer identification
   this severe on its own is enough to trigger the restriction-breach override (framework §6): band
   capped at Moderate Trust and Category 1 reduced ≥1 level, both applied in the trust-score file.

2. **ATR(14) is stated as an estimate of 78.50 (§11, §12, §19, §20) but the leak-free level file gives
   99.84 on a full-day basis — a 21.4% relative gap, inside the "discrepancy" band (10–25%) but close to
   the 25% Category-3-failure line (brief §4, ATR14 row).** Notably, 78.50 is much closer to
   `atr14_cash` (75.6621, a 3.75% gap) even though the report's own OHLC (and its "loco London,
   continuous" framing) checks out against `_full`, not `_cash` — i.e. the OHLC basis and the ATR basis
   are internally inconsistent with each other. A compliant report must either derive ATR(14) from the
   validated 5+ session close series and land within 10% of 99.84 (89.9–109.8) on the `_full` basis it
   otherwise uses, or explicitly compute and label it on the cash-session series and target 75.6621
   (68.1–83.2). This error understates every 0.25×ATR stop buffer and the 3×ATR runner cap used across
   all three cards.

3. **The 15-Jul Close is stated as 4,070.00 (§5, §6) against a level-file close of 4,060.37 — a gap of
   9.63, inside the "discrepancy" band (4.0–11.5, brief §4) but a genuine, disclosed methodological
   choice, not an error: §5 explicitly adopts the late-session read over its own $4,052.78 weighted
   median and explains why.** No action required beyond noting the gap; Open (diff 0.89), High (diff
   8.81) and Low (diff 0.06) all reconcile cleanly to `_full`.

4. **Daily pivots R1/R2/R3 (§12: 4,100.83 / 4,131.67 / 4,173.33) all fail the level-file check**, while
   S1/S2 (4,028.33 / 3,986.67) are consistent and P/S3 are discrepancies. Level-file `_full` values:
   R1 4,088.56 (diff 12.27), R2 4,116.75 (diff 14.92), R3 4,152.31 (diff 21.02) — all exceed the 11.48
   failure threshold (0.115×99.84). The R-side errors trace to the report's own composite High
   (4,090.00, itself a discrepancy vs the leak-free 4,081.19) and Close (defect 3) feeding the
   P=(H+L+C)/3 formula. A compliant daily pivot table recomputed from the level file's own H=4,081.19,
   L=4,017.44, C=4,060.37 gives P=4,053.00, R1=4,088.56, R2=4,116.75, R3=4,152.31, S1=4,024.81,
   S2=3,989.25, S3=3,961.06 — use these once the underlying 15-Jul OHLC is corrected.

5. **The 25-day swing high/low used throughout §9, §13 and Trade 3C (4,310.00 / 3,985.76) both fail
   badly against the level file's `swing_high_25d_full` (4,382.22, diff 72.22) and `swing_low_25d_full`
   (3,943.08, diff 42.68) — roughly 3–4× the failure threshold (19.97, brief §4, high/low row).** The
   stated 25-day low (3,985.76) is numerically identical to the report's own 5-day low, which suggests
   the lookback used to build §13's Fibonacci anchor and Trade 3's range boundary was never genuinely
   extended to 25 sessions despite §13's explicit claim of a 10-session extension. This is consequential
   because it feeds three downstream constructions: (a) §13's entire Fibonacci retracement grid is built
   from the wrong endpoints — recomputed from the correct 4,382.22 → 3,943.08 swing, the levels shift
   materially (e.g. the 0.618 level moves from 4,109.62 to 4,214.03); (b) Trade 3's range-boundary
   triggers (upside 4,329.63, downside 3,966.13) are both mis-calibrated against the true range; (c) see
   defect 8 for the compounding fork-selection error.

6. **§11 (VOLator table) mislabels its own arithmetic: "True ranges (last 4 sessions): 75.0 / 72.2 /
   116.96 / 72.5 → ATR(5) = 84.16" averages four values and calls the result "ATR(5)"** — a 5-period
   average needs five true ranges, not four. Either add the missing fifth true range or relabel this
   "ATR(4)". This is a minor labelling defect, separate from the estimation-methodology gap in defect 2.

7. **§17 Forecast is not the required single sentence.** Rule: brief §2 row 2.1 ("§17 Forecast (ONE
   sentence)") and row 4.4 ("§17 exactly one sentence, no hedge stacking"). The report's §17 is a
   two-row table plus a multi-sentence paragraph stacking several conditional clauses ("Directionally
   the forecast leans mildly constructive; structurally it does not contradict the bearish medium
   regime, because..."). Condense to one calibrated sentence stating the central estimate, direction and
   confidence (e.g. "Gold is expected to consolidate with a marginal upward drift toward ~4,085 over the
   next five sessions, Medium confidence at one session out, Low confidence beyond it given the 17-Jul
   Michigan risk.").

8. **Charts (spec §7) is entirely absent — no heading, no caption, no placeholder anywhere in the
   report.** Rule: brief §2 row 2.3 explicitly accepts a caption or placeholder as evidence given the
   .docx→.md image-drop, but that exemption presumes a Charts section exists to be converted; here there
   is none at all. Add a §7 Charts heading with at minimum a placeholder/caption per chart referenced
   elsewhere in the analysis.

## Card-level defects

11. **Trade 3's fork selection is wrong for its own stated regime.** §11 explicitly states the regime is
    RANGING (KER 0.117), and §21b's fork-selection note reasons "Regime is RANGING..., so 3A/3B
    trend-continuation forks do not apply" — but per the M5 construction rules (brief §3), only Trade 3A
    is the trend fork; **Trade 3B is the RANGE fork** and Trade 3C is reserved for TRANSITION regime.
    A RANGING regime should therefore have produced Trade 3B (limits at 11.4–21.4% / 78.6–88.6% of the
    25-day range, stops 10% of width inside, TP1 mid, TP2 far side −10%), not 3C's
    confirmed-close-beyond-boundary breakout construction. Using the corrected 25-day range from the
    level file (high 4,382.22, low 3,943.08, width 439.14): a compliant long limit sits at
    3,993.14–4,037.06 (11.4–21.4%), a compliant short limit at 4,288.24–4,332.16 (78.6–88.6%), stops
    43.91 inside the boundary, TP1 at the range mid 4,162.65, and TP2 at the far side minus/plus 10% of
    width (long TP2 = 4,338.31, short TP2 = 3,986.99).

12. **Trade 3's downside/upside triggers are also built on the wrong 25-day swing extremes** (defect 5):
    even setting the fork-selection error aside, 3,966.13 and 4,329.63 are both derived from swing
    values (4,310.00 / 3,985.76) that miss the level file's true 4,382.22 / 3,943.08 by 72.22 and 42.68
    respectively. Any reconstruction of Trade 3 — 3B as above, or a corrected 3C — must start from the
    level file's 25-day range, not the report's own stated one.

13. **Trade 2 Leg B and Leg A each state a stop-derivation formula that does not match the stop actually
    recorded.** Leg B's note derives "3,986.67 − 0.25×ATR(19.63) → 3,967.04" but the recorded stop is
    3,966.13 (diff 0.91). Leg A's note derives "4,131.67 + 0.25×ATR(19.63) → 4,151.30, rounded to
    swing-high cover" but the recorded stop is 4,151.88 (diff 0.58). Rule: brief §2 row 3.4 (internal
    reconciliation) — the stop shown in the card must equal the stop the card's own stated formula
    produces (adjusted only for the disclosed swing-high rounding on Leg A). Reconcile the two figures,
    and note that both derivations also use the understated ATR estimate (defect 2) — once ATR is
    corrected toward 99.84, the 0.25×ATR buffer widens from 19.63 to 24.96 on both legs.
