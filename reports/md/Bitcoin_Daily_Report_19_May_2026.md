# §5 Consensus Build Explanation
Six independent sources were normalized to a single USD/BTC spot quote at the 19 May 2026 00:00 UK anchor (effectively the late-NY / pre-Asia handover of 18 May).
- **Normalization.** All quotes are USD-paired spot. No stablecoin pairs (BTC/USDT, BTC/USDC) entered the consensus build — they remain disqualified by configuration. Aggregator quotes (CoinMarketCap, Bloomberg-wire) were taken as 24-hour mid; venue-direct quotes (Coinbase Pro page, Coinbase quote API as reported by The Crypto Times) were taken at the timestamps cited.
- **Weighting rationale.** Tier-1 exchange and tier-1 wire prints (Coinbase Pro, Bloomberg/Bloomingbit) received full weight. The CoinMarketCap volume-weighted consensus received partial weight as a corroborating aggregator. The Fortune morning quote was used to bracket but down-weighted because it samples a single Eastern-time minute, not the UTC handover. The CryptoBriefing narrative reference was excluded from the numerical median and used only as range context.
- **Exclusions.** Stablecoin-paired quotes excluded; wash-trade-prone unregulated venues excluded; CME BTC=F futures basis observed but not folded into spot consensus.
- **Why this number is defensible.** The five included quotes converge within a ~$880 window around $76,900, narrower than the 18 May intraday range itself ($1,988). Median is therefore well-defined and resilient to any single source. Confidence is set at Medium rather than High because the consensus snapshot follows an unusually heavy liquidation session, and the close-to-close volatility on 18 May was ~3.0% — above the trailing 14-session mean.

**Substitution / Spreads — Mixed**
Within crypto: BTC dominance rising vs ETH (ETH/BTC at 0.0274, near 2026 lows). Against external alternatives: gold trades near $4,569 / oz (gold is +1.5% on the week, BTC is −5%) — gold is winning the safe-haven flow during the Iran premium. Bond yields are elevated, raising the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset like BTC.
**Policy / Regulation — Mildly Supportive**
Senate Banking Committee advanced the CLARITY Act. NYDIG flagged a 'realistic passage window' from June to year-end. This is a structural-positive but currently absorbed by macro noise; the May 8–9 CLARITY rally was the regulatory tailwind expressing itself, and the 18 May breakdown shows that the bullish catalyst is currently overpowered by Iran/inflation.
**Macro / Logistics / FX — Negative**
Strait of Hormuz tensions are pushing Brent above $109 (a +3.3% Friday move). US 10-year yields are rising. DXY is firming toward 99.3. April CPI and PPI both came in hotter than expected — markets have fully priced out 2026 Fed rate cuts. All three macro channels (FX, rates, oil) are simultaneously BTC-negative.
**Near-term Catalysts — Several**
PCE inflation print (next week) is the principal scheduled macro event. Iran/Trump headline risk is the unscheduled but dominant catalyst — any Trump→Iran escalation or de-escalation tweet can move BTC by 2–3% within minutes. Strategy purchase announcements (typically Monday) are recurring spot demand events. ETF flow data is published daily — a sequence of negative-flow sessions amplifies the trend.
