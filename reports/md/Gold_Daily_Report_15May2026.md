# §12 Key Market Considerations
### Supply (structural)
Global mine production growth remains pinned to a 1–2% annual rate — the slowest growth phase among industrial commodities. No new major projects coming online in 2026. Refining throughput is steady. Tag: structural · price-supportive baseline (no near-term mover).
### Demand — central banks (structural)
World Gold Council projects 750–850 tonnes of central-bank net purchases in 2026 — historically exceptional. China (PBoC) believed to be buying off-books since its May 2024 disclosure pause; Poland adding 20+ tonnes YTD; India, Türkiye, Czechia all positive. Structural price floor consensus from sell-side strategists clusters around 4,500–4,600 USD/oz — i.e. ~50–150 USD below current spot. Tag: structural · price-supportive.
### Demand — physical retail (cyclical headwind)
India raised import tariffs on gold and silver from 6% to 15% mid-week (13–14 May). The move is explicitly aimed at curbing bullion purchases and supporting the rupee. Near-term Indian wedding-and-festival demand is dampened. Tag: cyclical · price-negative for 2–4 weeks (Indian retail elasticity is high in the short run, low in the long run).
### Substitution / Spreads
Gold/Silver ratio compressed from 62 (7 May) to ~53.5 (14 May) — silver outperforming sharply on industrial-demand re-rating following the US–China tariff truce. Platinum has lagged. The narrowing ratio is normal for risk-on / industrial-positive regimes; it does not threaten the gold thesis directly but it does signal that incremental precious-metals capital is flowing into silver, not gold. Tag: cyclical · price-negative on the margin.
### Policy / Regulation
Kevin Warsh narrowly confirmed as Fed Chair by the US Senate on 13 May — markets are reading him as marginally more hawkish than the predecessor framework. The 90-day US–China tariff truce (effective ~10–11 May) has cut US import duties from 145% to 30% on Chinese goods. Both items reduce the safe-haven premium on gold. Tag: cyclical · price-negative.
### Macro / Logistics / FX

Tag: cyclical · structurally split — real-yield negative for gold, war-risk-premium positive for gold; the real-yield side has dominated the past three sessions.
