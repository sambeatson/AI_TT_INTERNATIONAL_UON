Silver fell alongside gold and the gold–real-yield spread has widened against bullion. Elevated energy costs (Brent firm on Hormuz risk) keep the inflation-vs-rates calculus tilted toward 'higher-for-longer', which disadvantages gold versus yield-bearing alternatives in the short run.
## Near-term catalysts
