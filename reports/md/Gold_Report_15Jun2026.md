**Supply — neutral (structural).** Mine supply is inelastic over the horizon; official-sector accumulation is the supply-side story. The PBoC disclosed its 19th consecutive monthly purchase (a ~10-tonne May increase, per the World Gold Council / USAGOLD daily report), structurally price-supportive.

**Policy / regulation — price-negative (cyclical).** The ECB raised rates on 11 June (first hike since 2023) and lifted its inflation forecasts; the Fed is priced ~70% to hike later in 2026. A globally hawkish central-bank tilt is the dominant gold headwind.
**Macro / FX — price-negative (cyclical).** US May CPI at a three-year high and PPI +6.5% y/y reinforced rate-hike bets; the dollar (USDX) firmed and real yields rose. Both channels are gold-negative and outweigh gold's inflation-hedge premium in the short run.
**Near-term catalysts.** The FOMC decision on 17 June (within the 5-day horizon) is the single dominant catalyst. Also live: any US–Iran peace-deal headline (haven-bid sensitive) and the Philadelphia Fed manufacturing index. See §13d.
