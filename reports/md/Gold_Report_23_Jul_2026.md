# §5 Consensus Build Explanation
Eight observations were collected across six independent providers. Normalisation proceeded in three stages. Futures quotes were converted to spot-equivalent by removing the observed front-month premium, which ranged between 3 and 5 dollars per ounce across the capture window — a narrow and stable basis consistent with an unstressed physical market. Timestamps were then aligned: quotes captured before 12:00 UK on 22 July were treated as early-session and demoted, because the session's directional move occurred principally in the London afternoon and New York morning. Finally, all quotes were confirmed as loco London 400-ounce equivalent, so no purity or location adjustment was required.
Weighting favoured the four late-session spot observations, which cluster in a 16.35 dollar band between 4,135.20 and 4,151.55. The arithmetic mean of that cluster is 4,142.61; the median is 4,141.84. The consensus was set at 4,146.00, marginally above the midpoint, because the two most recently timestamped observations sit at the upper end of the cluster and the session was closing on its highs — a directional tie-break the evidence supports.
Two exclusions were made. The Investing.com capture at 4,078.95 was excluded from the consensus because it timestamps to 12:14, before the bulk of the day's advance, and retaining it would drag the consensus below every late-session observation. The 21 July close at 4,072.04 was retained only as a prior-session anchor. The resulting figure is the most defensible available because it rests on four independently sourced, contemporaneous, same-basis spot quotes rather than on any single venue, and because the futures corroboration at 4,144.00 basis-adjusted falls inside the spot cluster — cross-venue agreement that is the strongest available validation short of an LBMA fix.

Higher lows are in place across four consecutive sessions (3,965.65 → 4,003.72 → 4,049.13 → 4,049.60), and the 22 July close broke above every high in the window.
The counter-signal is momentum extension. RSI2 at 94.93 is an extreme reading, and it has now printed above 84 for two consecutive sessions. Nearest support in the five-day structure is the 21 July open at 4,049.13, which coincides closely with the 22 July session low of 4,049.60; that double-touch makes 4,049 the structurally significant floor. Nearest resistance is untested — price closed at the window high with no overhead structure inside the five-session block, so the first meaningful resistance comes from the pivot array (see §11).

## Short versus medium-term synthesis
The five-session read is Trending Bullish. The 25-session read is Transitional. Under the synthesis matrix, any transitional component forces the combined protocol to reduced conviction — wait for confirmation before committing full size. This is the governing constraint on everything in §21.
## Kaufman efficiency confirmation
The 13-session Kaufman efficiency ratio computes to −0.0051 after three-period smoothing. In absolute terms this is 0.005, far below the 0.13 trending threshold and also below the 0.09 inner band. The classification is Ranging — Neutral. The interpretation is important: the market has travelled a very long path to end up almost exactly where it started 13 sessions ago. Every dollar of directional progress has been paid for with roughly 200 dollars of round-trip movement. This directly contradicts the short-term trending-bullish read.
Where the two disagree, the efficiency read takes precedence for position sizing and the short-term read takes precedence for direction. The rationale: the efficiency ratio measures whether a move is likely to persist, and it is telling us clearly that recent moves in this market have not persisted. Direction may still be up over the next session, but the probability of a multi-day trend extension is low. Size accordingly.
## Volatility read
The scaled volatility series currently reads −0.57 against its own 25-session distribution, with a ten-observation slope of −0.20. Volatility is below its recent average and contracting. This is a ranging or transitional signature, not a trending one, and it independently corroborates the efficiency read. There is one important caveat: the 22 July true range of 102.50 dollars is a sharp single-session expansion that has not yet worked through the smoothed series. If the next one or two sessions sustain ranges above 80 dollars, the slope will flip positive and the regime read would need to be revisited.
## Consolidated regime
**Short-term trending bullish, medium-term transitional, efficiency ranging-neutral, volatility contracting. Under the mapping rule, the trending-up gate fails because the medium-term component is not trending and the efficiency classification is not trending-up. The range gate also fails, because the synthesis is not ranging-on-ranging and the efficiency and volatility gates cannot both be satisfied against a trending short-term read. The consolidated regime label is therefore TRANSITION — the default fall-through where signals disagree.**

# §12 Key Market Considerations
## Supply
- Mine supply is stable with no reported disruption at major operations. Supply is not a marginal price driver in the current window.
- Recycling flows typically increase after sustained high prices. With gold having spent much of 2026 above 4,000 and having peaked near 5,600 in January, scrap supply is likely running above trend — a mild structural headwind.
## Demand
- Central bank purchasing remains the structural pillar. Chinese official reserves rose to 2,313.46 tonnes from 2,306.30 in the latest reporting month, a seventh consecutive monthly increase. Indian reserves also edged higher to 880.52 tonnes. This is steady, price-insensitive accumulation.
- Physical demand is reported as leading the current rebound, with the gold-silver ratio breaking below 70 — consistent with genuine offtake rather than purely financial positioning.
- Investment demand is the swing factor and is currently conflicted, caught between geopolitical hedging and a rising real-yield environment.
## Policy
- The Federal Reserve is in its pre-meeting blackout ahead of next week's FOMC. Rates are expected to be held at 3.50–3.75%, with roughly 85.6% probability assigned to no change.
- The forward path is the live issue. Market pricing implies approximately 68–70% probability of a September increase, and two 25 basis-point hikes by March 2027. A hiking cycle restarting from here would be a material structural negative for bullion.
- US inflation printed 3.50% in June, down from 4.20% — disinflation is progressing, but oil above 90 dollars threatens to stall or reverse it, which is precisely why the hike probability has risen.
## Macro and geopolitical
- US strikes on Iranian targets have continued for eleven consecutive nights. President Trump has downplayed the likelihood of near-term talks and warned of further strikes.
- Houthi forces have announced a maritime embargo against Saudi Arabia and are disrupting Red Sea shipping. Reports of a possible ten-day truce to secure Strait of Hormuz routes represent the primary de-escalation scenario.
- Brent at 91.22 and WTI at 84.60 are both at or near one-month highs, feeding directly into the inflation expectations channel.
## Catalysts in the forecast window
- 23 July — US initial jobless claims. A soft print would ease hike expectations and support gold.
- 24 July — Flash manufacturing and services PMIs for July. Higher-priced components would reinforce the hawkish read.
- Next week — FOMC decision and statement. The statement language on the September path matters far more than the rate decision itself.
- Ongoing — any confirmed Iran truce or ceasefire announcement, which would likely trigger an immediate unwind of the geopolitical premium.
