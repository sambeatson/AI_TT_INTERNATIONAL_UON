adjustment is required. The only normalisation step is timing: the
freshest validated cash close is the 12 May print at 7,400.96, and this

relevant validated cash print and is treated as a recent corroborator.
The intraday range (7,338.54 -- 7,403.00) provides the dispersion needed
to set the indicative band.

Weighting follows the configured weighted-median method. Recency, source
credibility, and methodology transparency place the official S&P Dow
Jones index provider feed (via FRED) and the Yahoo Finance close at the
top tier. CNBC and TheStreet live-blogs are reported here because they
timestamp the freshest 12 May close ahead of FRED\'s next-day
publication; both agree to two decimals with Yahoo Finance, so they are
accepted as directly equivalent. Investing.com provides the intraday
open, high, and low used in the 5-day OHLC table. TradingView is an
aggregator and is treated as a directional comparable only --- it agrees
with FRED on the 11 May close.

The ES futures observation at ≈ 7,426 is held back as a directional-only
confirmation row, in line with the basis restriction that excludes
futures from cash OHLC. It is noted but not weighted in the consensus
number. No observations were excluded as outliers; the price-evidence
base is unusually tight for this asset class, reflecting deep
multi-source coverage. The consensus number of 7,401 therefore reflects
the 12 May validated cash close to the nearest integer, with the
7,375--7,430 indicative range capturing one full trading day\'s intraday
dispersion plus the prior-day reference high.
