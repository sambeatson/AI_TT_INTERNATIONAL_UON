**§5 Consensus Build Explanation**

Comparables were normalized to the cash-session close basis (16:00 ET)
for the official S&P Dow Jones reference. No FX adjustment is required
(USD-quoted). No freight, grade or quality adjustment applies to an
equity index --- only the cash-vs-futures distinction was screened, and
ES futures observations were down-weighted in favour of cash closes.
Weighting was applied as: (i) two-source corroboration mandatory for
every close; (ii) institutional / exchange-data tiers (S&P DJI via FRED,
Yahoo Finance \^SPX) weighted above media reports of identical numbers;
(iii) the most recent two sessions (13 May, 14 May) received highest
recency weight in the consensus build.

Exclusions: ES futures pre/post-market readings were excluded from the
cash-basis consensus (e.g., 7,477.25 ES futures on 14 May overnight
tape) --- these are useful for §10 cross-reads and §21 entry timing
only, not for the consensus price. The final number --- 7,501.59 --- is
the most defensible because it is corroborated by both a
percentage-based report (CNBC: +0.77%) applied to a separately-sourced
prior close (Yahoo: 7,444.25) and a level-based report (TheStreet: above
7,500 for the first time).
