# §5 Consensus Build Explanation

The consensus is built using a weighted-median across the six sources,
with the official S&P Dow Jones close (via FRED and CNBC) carrying full
weight and aggregator feeds (Yahoo Finance, Investing.com) carrying
partial weight to avoid over-counting the same upstream feed. CME ES
futures are treated as confirmation only and excluded from the
cash-basis median to prevent basis blending --- a key restriction of
this instance (no cash-and-futures mixing).

Three observations weighted most heavily: (i) the 18 May NYSE close at
7,403.05; (ii) the 15 May close at 7,408.50 as the most recent
corroborated week-end print; and (iii) the 14 May intraday peak at
7,517.12 as the structural reference high. Aggregator intraday tick data
(TradingView 7,391.60 at 12:18 ET) was excluded from the median as it
does not represent a settlement print and would skew the official close
anchor.

The final number --- 7,403.05 --- is the most defensible because it is
the latest exchange-settled cash close, corroborated across two
independent feeds and consistent to two decimal places with the index
provider\'s reported value.
