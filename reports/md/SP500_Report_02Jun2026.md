so no FX, freight, or specification conversion is required; the only
normalization is timing --- every quote is reconciled to the official
regular-session close. The weighted-median method gives top weight to
the index provider (S&P Dow Jones / FRED) and the exchange-corroborated

aggregator ranges (Investing.com) are treated as directional only and
excluded from the close determination. The 01 June record close of
7,599.96 is the most defensible current level because it is the latest
official regular-session print and is corroborated by independent
reporting of the same value and percentage move. No observations were
rejected as outliers; intraday open/high/low values for individual May
sessions that could not be matched by a second independent intraday feed
are flagged single-source indicative in §6 and are excluded from pivot
