The S&P 500 cash index enters the 03 July holiday close near **7,482.5
index points**, essentially unchanged on the final completed session (02

softer-than-expected June payrolls print that markets read as mildly

inflation; and (3) quarter-end/early-Q3 positioning after the strongest
quarter since 2020. Short-term technical read is **Indecision /
compression** --- the last two sessions printed narrow, overlapping

remains **Trending -- Bullish** off the 7,238 June low. The efficiency

efficiency. The composite regime is therefore **TRANSITION**.
Directional conviction is a marginal **LONG (score +0.25)**, carried by
the intact medium-term uptrend and positive efficiency bias but heavily
tempered by contracting volatility and a mild bearish news tilt. The
single most important watch item is the sustainability of the
semiconductor de-rating: a second leg lower in memory/AI names is the
