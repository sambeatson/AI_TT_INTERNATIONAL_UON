producers, defence primes, and industrials; consumer discretionary and
commercial REITs are guiding cautiously. Mega-cap concentration remains
high --- equal-weight S&P trails cap-weighted, signalling that
index-level optimism is still leadership-driven rather than broad.

Powell\'s chair tenure formally ends 15 May; Senate vote on Kevin
Warsh\'s nomination is sequenced for this week. Communication regime
change is itself a policy variable: Warsh has signalled fewer press
conferences and reduced forward guidance. The market is unwilling to
fully price a 2026 cut until the energy pass-through into core CPI is
settled.

Macro --- energy-driven inflation risk is the dominant macro overhang;
the Iran conflict has kept the Strait of Hormuz at \~80% reduced flow
for \~10 weeks, with crude near \$98/bbl. Labour data is firming ---
April NFP +115k beat +62k consensus, second consecutive monthly gain.

Catalysts --- Tuesday 12 May April CPI (8:30 ET) is the single most
market-moving event in the 5-day window. Wednesday 13 May April PPI
follows. Thursday 14 May April Retail Sales. Friday 15 May Powell\'s
chair term ends and University of Michigan preliminary sentiment is
