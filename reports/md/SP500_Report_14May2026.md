**§12 Key Market Considerations**

**Demand --- Price-supportive (mixed)**

Earnings demand remains the anchor. The Q1 2026 earnings season
delivered above-consensus operating margins across mega-cap tech, with
hyperscaler results lifted by AI capex monetisation. AAPL touched
\$300/share intraday on 12 May for the first time. The breadth picture
is weaker --- roughly two-thirds of S&P 500 constituents traded lower in
the 12 May session, with the index held up by Apple, Nvidia, Micron and
the semiconductor complex. Sustained leadership concentration is
structurally fragile but historically capable of carrying the index for
extended windows.

**Supply / Issuance --- Neutral**

No notable equity issuance overhang. Buyback announcements from the Q1
earnings season have set a strong tailwind for the May--July window. The
IPO pipeline is active but not at saturating levels.

**Policy --- Price-negative (short-term)**

April CPI printed at 3.8% YoY, above the 3.7% consensus, and the April
PPI release on 13 May printed +1.4% MoM --- the largest monthly increase
since March 2022, against a 0.5% consensus. Near-term Fed rate-cut
expectations have been substantially reduced; the rates curve has firmed
at the short end. This is a clear headwind to multiple-driven upside.
Source: BLS / Trading Economics.

**Macro and market structure --- Mixed**

Equity market structure remains constructive at the index level (VIX
17.9 --- below stress threshold of 20) but oil-led inflation pressure
and a firm USDX (\~98.6) are dual headwinds for multinational earnings
translation and goods-cost pressure. Crude (WTI \~\$101--102) is
elevated on Iran war supply concerns. Source: EIA, Trading Economics,
Yahoo Finance.

**Near-term catalysts --- Mixed (skewed to event risk)**

The Trump--Xi summit in Beijing during the 14 May session is the most
consequential single catalyst, with direct read-throughs to the
semiconductor complex (delegation includes Nvidia CEO). Expected to
produce headline-level guidance on advanced chip export policy. A
constructive readout would be powerfully bullish for tech-heavy indices;
a fractious readout would compound the inflation drag. PPI today and any
FOMC speakers later in the week are secondary.
