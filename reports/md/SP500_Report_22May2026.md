median. The 20 May official close of 7,432.97 is the firmest single
observation and is corroborated to the point by independent market
reporting. For the 21 May close basis, the weighted-median centre of the
close-basis observations sits at 7,400 index points, with the index
provider and exchange-tier sources carrying the highest weight and
aggregator/CFD references down-weighted. No normalization adjustments
are required because every observation is already in index points and
USD on the same cash-session basis.

point of the close-basis sources, it is consistent with the reported

equity-index tolerance; those fields are carried as single-source
indicative and are flagged throughout (see §6 and §19).

12\. Key Market Considerations

Demand for equity risk --- price-negative (cyclical)

The dominant near-term force is the back-up in long-dated US Treasury
yields toward multi-month highs, which raises the discount rate on
equity cash flows and lifts the appeal of competing fixed income. This
is an event-driven, reversible factor tied to the inflation and oil
narrative rather than a structural shift.

Earnings leadership --- neutral (structural undertone)

Mega-cap technology earnings are the structural engine of the index.
Nvidia\'s first-quarter print beat on the headline but its forward guide
failed to clear the top of the analyst range, leaving the AI-leadership
trade without fresh fuel. The structural earnings story is intact; the
cyclical catalyst is absent.

Energy and inflation pass-through --- neutral, recently volatile
(cyclical)

Crude oil spiked on Middle East supply fear, then reversed sharply lower
as a possible Iran de-escalation emerged. Lower oil eases the inflation
pass-through that had been pressuring equities, but the headline risk
remains two-sided and unresolved.

Macro / FX --- mild headwind (cyclical)

A broadly firm dollar is a modest translation headwind for multinational
earnings but is not the swing factor this week (see §10, §14).

Near-term catalysts

The five-day horizon carries a US core PCE inflation release, ongoing
Treasury supply/yield dynamics, and continued Middle East de-escalation
headlines. All three are flagged in §13d; core PCE is the highest-impact
scheduled item.

long-dated US Treasury yields toward multi-month highs. This
cross-references the §10 VIX read --- rising implied volatility and

Inflation. The inflation narrative is two-sided: oil\'s spike then sharp

within a single week. Net effect on the index is neutral pending the
core PCE release flagged in §13d.

Safe-haven / real yields. With long-end yields elevated, real yields are
firm --- historically a headwind for long-duration equity exposure such
as mega-cap technology, the index\'s leadership cohort. Price-negative
at the margin.

Liquidity and FX. Cross-referencing the §10 USDX read: the dollar is
broadly firm but not sharply trending. The translation headwind for
multinational earnings is modest and is not the swing factor this week.

Energy. Cross-referencing §10: crude\'s sharp reversal lower is, on
balance, supportive for equities because it eases the inflation
pass-through --- but the move is headline-driven and reversible.

Positioning. VIX in the high-teens with a rising slope indicates hedging
demand is picking up without signalling outright stress (the
configuration treats 20 as elevated and 30 as stressed; the current
reading sits below elevated). Positioning is cautious rather than
capitulatory.
