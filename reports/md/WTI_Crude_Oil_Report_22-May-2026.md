Resilient equity markets argue global growth expectations have not deteriorated — consistent with the cross-asset read in §10 that this is a premium unwind, not a demand collapse.

Policy / regulation — price-supportive on net (cyclical). The expiry of a US waiver on sanctions targeting Russian seaborne oil reimposes restrictions on a meaningful export stream — a tightening, price-supportive policy lever working against the geopolitical-premium unwind. The US–Iran negotiation track is itself the central policy variable.
Macro / logistics / FX — price-negative (cyclical). A firm dollar (see §10, §14) raises the cost of crude internationally and tightens conditions. The futures curve is in backwardation — front contracts priced well above deferred months — which signals near-term tightness even as the front month sells off.
Near-term catalysts — the dominant catalyst within the five-day horizon is the US–Iran negotiation headline flow and any formal statement on the Strait of Hormuz; the next EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report and any OPEC+ commentary are secondary. All are carried into §13d.
