Refinery crack spreads hit multi-year highs, hinting at firm product demand even as crude softens. Source: EIA Weekly / Reuters.
**Substitution / Spreads — neutral.** Brent–WTI held near its typical band; RBOB gasoline posted a 3-week high and the crack soared to a 4-year high, which triggered the 06 Jul short-covering bounce in crude. Heating oil firm. These product-led supports are the main near-term floor under flat price.
**Policy / Geopolitics — price-negative (cyclical, reversible).** The Strait of Hormuz has reopened and US–Iran talks in Doha are progressing; the risk premium that carried crude to ~$95 in early June has largely evaporated. This is the dominant repricing driver — and the most reversible: any breakdown in talks or fresh Hormuz disruption is the primary upside tail.
**Macro / FX — mixed.** A soft June payroll (+57k) pushed the dollar below 101 and trimmed Fed-hike odds; a weaker USD is normally crude-supportive but is being overwhelmed here (see §10, §14).
**Near-term catalysts.** EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wed 08 Jul), FOMC June minutes (this week), and the next EIA STEO all fall inside the 5-session horizon — each can move the consensus (see §13d).
