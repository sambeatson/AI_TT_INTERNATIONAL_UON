## Substitution / spread — NEUTRAL
Brent traded near $88.5 with WTI near $86, a Brent-WTI spread around $2.5 that is consistent with the global-vs-US balance; no acute crack-spread distortion. The narrow spread signals the move is global (de-escalation) rather than a US-specific oversupply.
## Policy / geopolitics — price-NEGATIVE (was the premium)
The US-Iran framework (reported 60-day deal, 14-point memorandum, frozen-asset issue as the final obstacle) and Hormuz reopening commitments are removing the war premium that had carried WTI above $100. SPR posture is a latent lever but not active here.
## Macro / market structure — mildly SUPPORTIVE
A softer dollar (USDX ~99.8) and falling oil-linked inflation expectations ease financial conditions, a marginal support that the market is currently ignoring in favour of the supply story.
Upcoming catalyst flag (see §13d): the EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report and any signed US-Iran text are the near-term movers. Structural factor (6+ months): 2026 oversupply. Cyclical factor (reversible): the geopolitical risk premium, which can re-expand violently if talks collapse.
